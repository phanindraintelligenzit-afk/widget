"Good afternoon everyone. Today I am going to present DPI-LS. First, I will explain what DPI-LS is and why we built it."

What is DPI-LS?

"DPI-LS stands for Digital Performance Index – Life Science. It is a measurement engine that measures AI-agent performance."
  • What it is: A specialized platform to track and score Digital Workers (AI Agents).
  • Why we built it: Life Science companies need strict proof that AI is safe, compliant, and actually productive before using it.

Benefits & Advantages

"It helps us understand performance, quality, risk, governance, validation, and cost before we trust and scale an AI agent."
  • Trust & Safety: Strict governance rules block unsafe AI actions automatically.
  • Clear Measurement: Replaces guesswork with hard numbers across 7 specific categories (P, Q, E, G, R, V, C).
  • Human Oversight: Managers can easily review AI decisions and rate them.
  • Scalability: Safely deploy hundreds of agents with a central dashboard to monitor them all.

Open Live Dashboard

3. Onboarding & Configuration

"The user can onboard an AI agent, and provide its configuration."
  • Register a new AI with Business and Technical owner details.
  • Configure the exact weight (percentages) for each performance metric to match business goals.

4. Rating & Human Oversight

"...check its rating and score..."
  • Managers can submit performance reviews securely.
  • SME (Subject Matter Experts) can provide 1-to-5 star ratings that directly impact the AI's Quality score.

5. Profile & Resources

"...view the agent profile, and check the connected resources."
  • Profile: See historical execution scores, bands, and the full Manager Review timeline.
  • Resources: Access architectural manuals and operational guidelines.
