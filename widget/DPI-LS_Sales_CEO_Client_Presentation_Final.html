Good afternoon everyone. Today I am going to present DPI-LS. DPI-LS is a scoring dashboard that tells us exactly how well, how safely, and how cost-effectively our AI agents are working in the real world. – Life Science. It is a measurement engine that measures AI-agent performance. It helps us understand performance, quality, risk, governance, validation, and cost before we trust and scale an AI agent.

The business need

Why DPI-LS?

AI agents can do the work. Enterprises need to know how well they do it.
• Is the agent performing well?
• Are the results reliable?
• Are risks and policies under control?
• What does the work cost?
• Is the agent ready to scale?

02 / 10
The product

What is DPI-LS?

DPI-LS connects to an AI agent, collects its evidence, measures it, and produces a score.
AI Agent→Connect→DPI-LS Engine→Score→Dashboard
The agent performs the work. DPI-LS connects through APIs, MCP or telemetry, collects the required information, calculates the measurements and shows the result. In simple words: the agent does the work, DPI-LS measures it, and the dashboard shows the result.
03 / 10
The measurement model

Seven dimensions

One framework. Seven questions.
PProductivityIs useful work getting done?
QQualityAre results correct and reliable?
EExecutionAre actions completing successfully?
GGovernanceAre policies and controls followed?
RRiskWhat risks or incidents are present?
VValidationDoes output meet required rules?
CCostWhat does the AI work cost?
DPI-LS measures seven important areas: Productivity, Quality, Execution, Governance, Risk, Validation and Cost. These are combined into the overall DPI-LS score.

Product journey

How the user uses DPI-LS

A simple workflow from login to decision.
Login→Dashboard→Onboard Agent→Configuration→Rating / Score→Profile→Resources→Sign Out
First, the user logs in with a username and password. After login, the dashboard is displayed. The user can onboard an AI agent, provide configuration, check its rating and score, view the agent profile, check resources, use search and help/chatbot, and finally sign out.

From score to action

The score is explainable

DPI-LS does not stop at one number. It shows where the agent is strong or weak.
Overall DPI-LS score→Dimension scores→Evidence / signals→Improvement action
The important point is that DPI-LS does not only give one number. It also shows the dimension-level breakdown, so we can understand where the agent needs improvement and make a better decision.
