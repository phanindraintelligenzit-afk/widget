--- Slide 8 ---

APPENDIX · THE CONCEPTUAL INNOVATION
The Agent Performance Contract
Before an enterprise agent receives responsibility, define:

01
What outcome is expected?

02
What quality is acceptable?

03
What execution reliability is required?

04
What cost / token budget is acceptable?

05
What security thresholds apply?

06
Which governance policies are non-negotiable?

07
What conditions require human escalation?

THEN
The scoring engine measures the agent against the contract.
We establish a measurable performance contract for every enterprise agent.
