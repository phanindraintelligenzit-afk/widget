Why Do We Need Independent Resources?

Chandra is the Worker. DPI-LS is the Appraisal System.

The Problem of Self-Bias:

  • An AI cannot objectively grade its own homework. Asking an AI if it hallucinated yields unreliable results.

AWS APIs are NOT "Third Party":

  • They are our direct window into the client's cloud. Chandra knows nothing in advance; every fact is pulled fresh at task time.

Why Independent Witnesses?

  • Without external observability tools (DeepEval, Zipkin, LangSmith), the DPI score means nothing to a buyer. Independence makes the score sellable.

Chandra — The Worker (5-Step Procedure)

The Brain: Claude on AWS Bedrock | The Toolbelt: AWS APIs (boto3)

  • 1. Classify: What kind of problem is this? (Cost, Security, Compliance, etc.)
  • 2. Investigate: Pull actual facts from AWS APIs. Real numbers, no guessing.
  • 3. Assess Blast Radius (The Judgment Step):
    • Before acting, what breaks? E.g., refusing to kill an expensive GxP-validated genomics workload to save money. This is judgment, not automation.
  • 4. Recommend: Propose action + full reasoning (the safe move, not just the cheap one).
  • 5. Audit Summary: Writes everything it saw, thought, and decided into the work register (the Witness).

DPI — The Judge (Appraisal System)

DPI never asks Chandra how well it did. It reads independent evidence only.

  • Feed 1: Work Register
    • Every task, decision, and outcome Chandra logged (Feeds Productivity, Quality, Execution, Governance, Risk).
  • Feed 2: The AWS Bill
    • Actual $ Chandra costs to run (Feeds Cost).
  • Feed 3: The Answer Key
    • Were recommendations actually correct vs ground truth? (Feeds Validation).
  • DPI Engine & Scoreboard:
    • Connectors pull feeds, formula computes:
      PI = (P × Q × 1.5E) + (G × 1.5R) + (C × V)
    • Result: One trusted number. No human types a score in.
